# NNNN: Title in one line

- **Status**: Proposed
- **Date**: YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders**: who owns the decision
- **Related**: links to specs, PRs or other ADRs

## Context

What makes a decision necessary. The forces at play, the constraints, the state of the system today. Facts only, no solution yet.

## Options considered

1. **Option A**: what it means, its cost, its risk.
2. **Option B**: same.
3. **Option C** (chosen): same.

## Decision

What we do, in the present tense, in one or two paragraphs. Enough that a new engineer can apply it without asking.

## Consequences

- **Positive**: what gets easier.
- **Negative**: what gets harder, and the debt we accept knowingly.
- **Follow ups**: the specs, tests or migrations this decision forces.
